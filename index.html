How I work

Four principles the case studies keep demonstrating

01

Start with what cannot move

Law, policy, security, procurement, timeline, budget, organisational structure. On CRCS the immovable thing was a statute, not a system. On the 8th CPC it was a date set in legislation. Naming it early is what makes the rest of the plan honest.

02

State the decision and its cost

A decision without a stated trade-off is a preference. Deferring IVFRT's single data store cost analytical capability and bought port stability — written down that way, it can be reviewed rather than discovered later.

03

Give AI authority only as fast as evidence allows

Shadow, then a limited window, then production. Advisory output, a named human approving anything consequential, and every claim citing its source. Acceptance measured against a golden dataset, not a generic benchmark.

  • Shadow
  • Limited authority
  • Expanded window
  • Production

04

Measure the system, not the deliverable

“Delivered successfully” is not a result. Processing time, availability, profitability, adoption, defect rate, operational workload — every number on this site is one of those answers.
