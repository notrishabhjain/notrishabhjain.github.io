How I deliver

Four habits of an AI project manager

  1. Plan around what cannot move

    A law, a date, an audit window. I find the fixed constraint first and build the critical path around it.

  2. Govern what I do not control

    Ministries, vendors and agencies with no reporting line: one dependency log with an owner per item, and escalation agreed in advance.

  3. Put AI in only where it can be measured

    Agree the acceptance test first, compare the output with people, and keep a named person accountable for every decision.

  4. Stay through live operations

    Go-live is the start. I keep the backlog, releases and change requests honest once real users arrive.
