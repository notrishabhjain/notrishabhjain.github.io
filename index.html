Where I use AI

AI helps, but a person stays responsible

In government work a wrong answer can have legal and financial consequences. These are the three rules I follow.

Set limits first
Scoped · Phase 2 in procurement

What may it decide, and who answers?

On the green hydrogen portal the rule is simple. AI advises, a named officer approves every important action, and every claim points to its source document.

Test it against people
Live · measured on other programmes

Check it before anyone relies on it

On other programmes, translation in 22 languages and automated website scoring were compared with human reviewers, and scoring landed within ±10%, before they were trusted.

Earn more trust over time
In production

Watch, then a few hours, then a full day

On the wind-energy unit AI started by watching, then worked a six-hour window, then full days, one stage at a time. It was never rolled back.
