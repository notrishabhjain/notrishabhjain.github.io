About

Engineer first, then consultant, now project manager

I spent my first six years writing production software: mainframe automation for a UK bank, then blockchain systems. At EY I moved into government consulting, authoring the DPRs and RFPs for national platforms and holding vendors to them. Since 2024 I have been accountable for whole programmes, first an enterprise portfolio at Infosys and now national platforms for central government at BDO India.

That path is the point. I can read an architecture, run a steering committee and own a P&L. AI is now central to the work: I scope it where it earns its place, measure it against people before anyone relies on it, and keep a named person accountable for every decision.
