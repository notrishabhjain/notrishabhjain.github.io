What I own

The whole delivery surface

On every recent engagement, these sat with me, not with a steering committee or a vendor.

Scope

A signed baseline, then change control

Requirements agreed and signed before the first sprint; every change after that goes through a written, costed request.

Schedule

Plans built around what cannot move

Statutory dates, audit windows and launch commitments first; parallel streams and re-plans when a dependency slips.

Budget

P&L and commercials

Portfolio profit and loss, scoped and charged change work, and costed proposals, DPRs and RFPs.

People

Teams of 8 to 35

Delivery and support teams, consultants and vendor staff, with the Product Owner and Scrum Master roles when needed.

Vendors and agencies

Many parties, no shared reporting line

Steering committees, a named owner for every dependency, and escalation paths agreed before they are needed.

Risk and quality

Audits passed before launch

Internal security reviews ahead of external audits, phased UAT with real users, and AI scoped with a named person accountable.
