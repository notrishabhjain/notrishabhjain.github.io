About

I build the bridge between what an organisation needs to achieve and what technology can responsibly deliver.

Manager, Digital Transformation at BDO India, working with NeGD under MeitY. Based in Delhi, open to relocation and to remote work.

My career began in engineering and blockchain architecture. It grew into programme delivery, product ownership, technology consulting and transformation leadership.

Today I work where applied AI, digital public infrastructure, enterprise technology and programme execution meet. I turn complex requirements into products that operate at scale and stand up to regulatory, security and operational scrutiny.

AI is the next step along that line, not a change of direction. It is the same question I have been answering since the mainframe work: what is the system actually allowed to decide, and who answers for it?

Technology transformation succeeds when the product, operating model, governance and technology architecture are designed together.

What I do

Four areas

01

Applied AI and AI product delivery

04

Programme and portfolio leadership

Multi-vendor delivery across government and enterprise stakeholders, in agile and waterfall settings, with P&L and commercial accountability attached.
