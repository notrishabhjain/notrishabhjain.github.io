Senior Technical Program Manager, Payments Platform

About us
We are a fast-growing fintech building real-time card payments and settlement infrastructure for banks across APAC.

About the role
You will own end-to-end delivery of our payments platform roadmap, working with engineering, product, risk and compliance teams.

What you'll do
- Drive cross-functional programs from planning through launch, managing dependencies across multiple engineering teams
- Own program governance: risk, issue and change management, RAID logs and executive status reporting
- Partner with C-suite stakeholders and external vendors, including card schemes and processors
- Manage budgets and resource plans, and report delivery KPIs to leadership
- Lead incident retrospectives and drive improvements to MTTR and platform reliability
- Champion Agile/Scrum practices and continuous improvement

What we're looking for
- 8+ years of program management experience, including 3+ years in payments or financial services
- Proven track record managing multiple vendors and complex, multi-team programs
- Strong understanding of APIs, microservices and cloud platforms (AWS preferred)
- Experience with PCI DSS and regulatory compliance in a regulated environment
- Excellent written and verbal communication skills
- Hands-on experience with Jira and Confluence

Nice to have
- PMP or PgMP certification
- Familiarity with chargebacks, dispute management and reconciliation
- Exposure to Kafka or event-driven architecture

Benefits
Health insurance, flexible working, and visa sponsorship is not available for this role.
