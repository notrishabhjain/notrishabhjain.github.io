SENIOR PROJECT MANAGER - APPLICATION MANAGED SERVICES

Position Summary
Our client, a Fortune 500 energy company, is looking for a Senior Project Manager to lead an onsite-offshore application support and enhancement portfolio of 60+ applications.

Responsibilities:
* Own SLA performance and service delivery for L2/L3 application support, with 24x7 coverage for critical systems
* Lead a distributed team of 30+ engineers across India and the US; handle resourcing, hiring and performance management
* Run ITIL-aligned incident, problem and change management; drive down MTTR and recurring incidents
* Manage P&L for the account, including budgeting, forecasting and margin improvement
* Identify automation opportunities (CI/CD, scripting, monitoring) to reduce manual effort
* Build trusted relationships with client CIO and application owners; run QBRs and governance meetings
* Grow the account through proactive proposals and new business

Requirements:
* 12+ years in IT services with 5+ years managing application support or AMS engagements
* Demonstrated experience turning around underperforming accounts
* Working knowledge of ServiceNow, Azure DevOps and Git
* ITIL Foundation certification required
* Experience in renewable energy or utilities domain is a plus
