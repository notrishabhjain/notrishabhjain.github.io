Product Owner (B2B SaaS)

Who we are: A Series C B2B SaaS company helping enterprises automate compliance workflows.

The role
We're looking for a Product Owner to own the backlog for our workflow engine and certification module.

In this role, you will:
- Translate product strategy and roadmap into well-groomed epics, user stories and acceptance criteria
- Lead sprint planning, backlog refinement, sprint reviews and retrospectives with a cross-functional Scrum team
- Conduct user research and customer interviews to inform prioritization and discovery
- Define and track success metrics and product analytics after launch
- Work with UX designers in Figma on prototypes and usability testing
- Write clear functional specifications for complex state machine and audit trail requirements

You have:
- 5+ years as a Product Owner, Product Manager or Business Analyst in Agile teams
- CSPO, PSPO or CSM certification
- Experience with Jira, Confluence and Miro
- Comfort with ambiguity and a bias for action
- SQL and basic data analysis skills

Bonus points if you have experience with GDPR, SOC 2 or audit-heavy products.
