Job Title: Program Manager – Digital Public Infrastructure

Overview
A leading consulting firm is hiring a Program Manager to support a central government ministry on a national e-governance platform under the Digital India programme.

Key Responsibilities
1. Manage end-to-end delivery of the national portal, from DPR and RFP drafting through vendor onboarding, development oversight and go-live
2. Coordinate with MeitY, NIC and multiple implementation agencies; maintain the project plan, risk register and milestone tracker
3. Run the PMO: weekly progress reporting to the Joint Secretary, steering committee packs and MIS dashboards
4. Ensure compliance with GIGW 3.0, WCAG 2.1 and STQC/CERT-In security audit requirements before launch
5. Drive business process re-engineering, as-is/to-be analysis and change management with state departments
6. Oversee UAT, training and phased rollout across states

Eligibility Criteria
- B.Tech/B.E. with MBA preferred
- Minimum 10 years of experience, with at least 5 years managing large government IT projects
- Strong knowledge of GFR, GeM procurement and CVC guidelines
- Experience with Aadhaar, DigiLocker or other India Stack integrations is desirable
- Excellent stakeholder management with senior government officials

Good to have: PMP or PRINCE2 certification, experience with NIC MeghRaj cloud hosting.
