## Notes

- The blockchain certifications are from the earlier part of your career and I've grouped
  and labelled them as such rather than hiding them. They're doing a specific job:
  substantiating pillar 2 (technical depth is real, not claimed). Dated certifications
  presented honestly as history read as credible; the same certifications presented as
  current read as padding.
- The four certificate images currently on the site are ~2.2 MB of JPEGs rendered at
  thumbnail size. If we keep them visible at all, they get compressed and served
  responsively. My recommendation: text entries with verification links, no images. The
  lightbox gallery of certificate screenshots is a strong junior-candidate signal, and
  you are not a junior candidate.
- `[VERIFY]` — is the Certified Scrum Master certification currently active? CSM requires
  renewal every two years, and a lapsed cert stated flatly on a public page is the kind
  of small inaccuracy that costs disproportionate credibility if someone checks.
