## Context

The Central Registrar of Cooperative Societies oversees multi-state cooperative
societies — a constituency measured in tens of millions of members across the country.
The work was moving a paper and part-digital process onto a national platform.

## The problem

Scale changes the nature of a requirement. A form that's merely awkward is a support
burden at ten thousand users and a national news story at forty million. And the people
using it aren't a self-selecting technical audience — they're society members and
officials across every state, on every kind of connection and device.

## What I owned

As Senior Consultant on the programme I worked across business process re-engineering,
functional requirement analysis, and implementation oversight — the As-Is mapping, the
gap analysis, and the To-Be design that had to reconcile what the rules required with
what the process could actually sustain.

Alongside CRCS I worked on Aaklan, NPI, and IVFRT — national platforms across multiple
ministries — running the same BPR and requirement analysis discipline, and I oversaw
security compliance across 90+ ministry websites.

their full titles or sponsoring ministries. Send me those and I'll write them properly;

## Outcome

A citizen-facing platform serving 40M+ users, and the governance around it: risk logs,
issue tracking, vendor performance monitoring, milestone dashboards, and reporting that
went to Secretaries and Additional Secretaries.

## What I'd point to

Working directly with Secretary and Additional Secretary level on approvals and scope
decisions. Not as a name-drop — because it's the specific skill of compressing a complex
technical trade-off into the two minutes of attention a decision-maker at that level can
give it, and being right. That skill transfers to any executive stakeholder, in any
sector, in any country.
