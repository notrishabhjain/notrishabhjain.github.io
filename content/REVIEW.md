## The two things that would most improve this site

**1. A people number from the Infosys turnaround.** The MTTR figure — four days to under
twelve hours — is your best hard number, and it proves you can fix a process. A retention
or attrition figure alongside it would prove you can fix a *team*, which is what a hiring
manager is actually buying at portfolio level. Even "kept 28 of 30 through the
transition" would carry more weight than another paragraph of description.

**2. Recent international exposure.** You're targeting international roles, and right now
every non-India data point on the site is from your TCS years. Anything more recent —
a stakeholder group, a global client, a distributed team across time zones — materially
changes how a London or Singapore recruiter reads this page.

Neither blocks the build. Both raise the ceiling.
