## Notes on this screen

- **No photo slider, no typed job titles.** Both were on the old site. Both read as
  filler to a senior reader, and filler is the one thing an eight-second screen cannot
  afford.
- **"I read the code and I sit in the steering committee"** is the most important
  sentence on the site. It's the whole positioning in eleven words, and it's the line
  a recruiter will repeat to a hiring manager. If it doesn't sound like you, tell me —
  but I'd fight to keep the idea even if we change the words.
- **The availability line goes above the fold**, not in the footer. You said you're
  actively looking, open to conversations, and treating this as a standing asset. The
  only one of those three that needs to be visible immediately is the first.
