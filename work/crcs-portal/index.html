Case study 04 · Ministry of Cooperation · Senior Consultant · 2022–24

CRCS Portal

A paper-based national registry whose real blocker was the MSCS Act, not the software — so the statute was amended first and the platform built second.

  • Statutory blocker
  • Change the constraint
  • Accountability by design
  • ~40M members

01 — Context

A national registry, on paper

The Central Registrar of Cooperative Societies, under the Ministry of Cooperation, holds the country's record of multi-state cooperative societies. The process was largely paper-based, covering roughly 1,500 multi-state societies and some 40 million members.

02 — Challenge

The blocker was not the software

The easy reading was that this was a technology-modernisation problem. It was not. The MSCS Act recognised neither electronic notice nor a signature that was not physical — so the intended digital workflow had no legal standing, however well it was built.

A platform delivered on that foundation would have been a platform nobody could lawfully use.

03 — Mandate

Support the transformation from process discovery and DPR and RFP authorship through ministry-side delivery oversight — including vendor scope discipline, deviation management and escalation against my own specification.

04 — Critical decisions

Decision 01

Change the constraint, not just the software.Why. I proposed amendments to the MSCS Act so the intended digital workflow could operate within an appropriate legal framework. The amendments were tabled and enacted in about six months. No amount of product work would have substituted for this.

Decision 02

Design accountability into the workflow.Why. A digitised paper process inherits the paper process's ability to stall silently. Role-based access per officer and automatic escalation at five days, seven days and fifteen days made delay visible by default rather than on request.
  • Role-based access per officer
  • Escalation at 5 days
  • Escalation at 7 days
  • Escalation at 15 days

05 — Delivery

Authoring the specification, then holding delivery to it

I authored the DPR and the RFP, and subsequently worked on the ministry side of my own specification. That is an unusual and useful position: the person asking whether a deviation is acceptable is the person who wrote what was asked for.

06 — Outcome

~40Mmembers on the live platform
~1,500multi-state societies
70%+reduction in application processing time
~6 moto table and enact the amendments

A paper-based national office became a live platform. The platform now holds the country's multi-state society record, including the Sahara credit cooperative society data used for refund validation.

07 — What this demonstrates

Transformation where software alone cannot solve the problem: identifying that the real constraint was statutory, getting the statute amended inside six months, designing accountability into the replacement workflow, and then holding a vendor to a specification I had written myself.
