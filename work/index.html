Senior Consultant · EY · 2022–24
150+parameters

G20 government-website benchmark

A national framework for ranking government websites, built with IIT Delhi and IIM Indore — now India’s official assessment.

The short version
Problem
No objective way to compare government websites on content, security, accessibility and citizen engagement; departments being assessed didn’t report to the team.
What I did
Designed a 150+ parameter framework with two top academic institutes, and checked the automated scoring against 20 human auditors before anyone relied on it.
Result
~100 sites assessed; scores tuned to within ±10% of auditors. India placed among the top five G20 economies in the benchmark, and the framework continues as the national assessment. ~$575K programme.
Senior Consultant · EY · 2022–24
22languages

National Portal of India v2.2

One front door to 20+ government information sources — a ~$2.6M programme.

The short version
Problem
A static directory of disconnected portals. The aim: reliable information from many sources, in one place, in 22 languages.
What I did
Wrote the DPR and RFP and onboarded the vendor. Treated machine translation as something to measure: human review, agreed language criteria and blind volunteer surveys.
Result
~80% of machine output accepted as translated, from an early error rate near one sentence in ten. Search and summaries planned for the next phase.
Senior Consultant · EY · 2022–24
1platform

National visa & immigration platform

Visa, e-visa and overseas-citizen services consolidated onto one national system across airports, land ports and seaports.

The short version
Problem
Old, separate systems with data-sync problems, and practice that differed from port to port.
What I did
Designed from watching officers and travellers work, not from the written spec. Kept separate databases in phase one, because a port has no maintenance window.
Result
One consolidated platform in staged rollout: seaport pilot, seaports live, airports piloting, land ports next.
