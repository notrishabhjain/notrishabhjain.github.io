NeSDA benchmarking framework

A benchmark across 80+ departments that did not report to the programme team.

80+departments
±10%vs human auditors
The short version

Situation

NeSDA is the Digital India awards and website-ranking framework. It promotes harmonised, current technology across government websites. The departments it assessed did not report to the programme team.

My call

Borrow the institution's authority instead of relying on persuasion: Secretary-level communication, CERT-In direction and a named nodal officer in each organisation. Validate the automated AHP scoring against human auditors before trusting it.

Outcome

Around 100 sites assessed with 20 auditors. Cases outside the band were noted, the outlier areas modified and the parameters tuned, until the scores sat within ±10% of the auditors. India placed among the top five G20 economies in the benchmarking, and the framework continues as AKAM (Aanklan).

Senior Consultant · 2022–24

IVFRT 2.0

Visa, e-visa, OCI and NRI services consolidated onto one national platform.

1platform
Seaportslive
The short version

Situation

Old, siloed and slow systems with data-sync problems, serving foreign nationals and tourists at the border, with local practice that differed from port to port.

My call

Design from observation of officers and travellers, not from the written specification. Defer collapsing the siloed databases into one store, because a port has no maintenance window and the change carried the most risk to stability.

Outcome

One consolidated platform in staged rollout: a two-seaport pilot, seaports live, airports piloting and land ports to follow.

Senior Consultant · 2022–24

National Portal of India v2.0

One digital front door over 20+ government sources, in 22 languages.

22languages
~80%output accepted
The short version

Situation

A static directory of disconnected portals. The aim was to bring authentic information from many government sources and APIs into one central view, in 22 languages.

My call

Treat translation as a measurable quality problem, not a model choice: human-in-the-loop review, accuracy measured against a pre-approved set of language parameters, and random blind surveys with volunteers. Graduate the review by traffic and measured quality. Build a custom CMS for the heterogeneous sources.

Outcome

About 80% of machine output accepted as translated, from an initial error rate near one sentence in ten. Semantic search and summarisation were specified for the next phase.

Senior Consultant · 2022–24

Skillchain

A blockchain credential platform taken to a unit-wide EY pilot.

~80%voluntary uptake
The short version

Situation

Credentials issued as NFTs on Quorum for an EY unit. Adoption was voluntary, so the product had to earn its own use.

My call

Gather requirements from all four stakeholder groups before building, and cut the NFT marketplace to protect the core.

Outcome

Around 80% voluntary uptake. The capability continued through 2024 and now lives in EY Skills and EY Badges on Degreed.

Blockchain Architect · de facto Product Owner · 2021–22

Mainframe automation and blockchain POCs

Reconciliation automation for a UK bank, worth about £200,000 a year.

~£200Ka year, client-calculated
The short version

Situation

A manual Visa chargeback reconciliation on a legacy Unisys environment. The blocker was a permission that had never been configured.

My call

Resolve the permission commercially instead of engineering around it, and rebuild the pipeline instead of scripting over the manual steps.

Outcome

About £200,000 a year saved by the client's own calculation, and 40% fewer reported defects at six months. Two blockchain proofs of concept reached production, and I set up the first blockchain lab inside TCS infrastructure.
