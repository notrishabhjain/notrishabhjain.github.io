EY India · 2022–24 · Senior Consultant

National benchmarking framework for government websites

Project-managed a 150+ parameter national framework for assessing government websites, built with academic research partners and now used to gate new sites before go-live.

In national useOperated as the national ranking standard for government websites.

Reach
80+ departments
Partners
Academic research institutions
Authority
None over the departments
150+parameters, objective by design
±10%automated score against human auditors
The delivery record

The brief

Create a defensible national way to assess and rank government websites, and get every department to act on its score.

The constraint

The departments being assessed did not report to the project, and each had reasons to contest a low score.

What I did

Routed authority rather than negotiating department by department: a senior mandate through the national cyber-security agency, and a named officer in each department accountable for closing findings. The automated scoring was calibrated against human auditors before anyone relied on it.

The outcome

About 100 sites assessed with 20 auditors, with automated scores held within ±10% of theirs. Results were reported at the top of the civil service, and the framework now gates new government sites before go-live.

Footprint
Air, land and sea ports
Rollout
Pilot first, then by port type
1national platform in place of port-level systems
3port types on one standard
The delivery record

The brief

Replace slow, separate systems, with data-sync problems and practice that differed from port to port, with one national platform.

The constraint

The work touches national security, a port has no maintenance window, and no port reports to another.

What I did

Managed the consultant team and designed from watching officers and travellers at work, not from the written specification. Kept the separate databases in phase one, because merging them carried the most risk for a system that cannot stop.

The outcome

One consolidated platform, rolled out in stages from a pilot, with the common standard adopted because it had been shown to work. Detail stays limited for security reasons.
