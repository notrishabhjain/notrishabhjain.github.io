FAQ

Common questions

Are you available full-time or freelance?

Both. I’m open to full-time remote roles with international teams, and to selected contract, fractional or fixed-scope engagements where the scope and timeline are clear.

Which time zones can you cover?

I’m in New Delhi (UTC+5:30). I overlap a full business day with the Gulf, most of the day with Europe, the UK and Singapore, and can cover US East Coast mornings by arrangement.

What size of programme fits best?

Anything from a 30-day launch to multi-million, multi-vendor programmes. I’ve led teams of 8 to 35 and coordinated work across ministries, vendors, cloud providers and auditors.

Can you work inside our tools and process?

Yes — Jira, Confluence, MS Project, Azure DevOps-style boards, Scrum, Kanban or a hybrid. I adapt to your governance rather than imposing mine.

How do you handle confidentiality?

Happy to sign an NDA before any detailed discussion. You’ll notice client names are withheld on this site where contracts require it.

How do we start?

Send a short note with the goal, the date and what’s blocking it. I’ll reply within two working days with questions or a suggested first call.
